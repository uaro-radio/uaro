UARO — Ukrainian Amateur Radio OperatorsUROUkrainian AmateurRadio Operators

Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators is a non-governmental organisation. We bring radio amateurs together, advance radiosport and technical creativity, and produce guides and reference materials.

Join UAROHandbook

Our mission

Our purpose is to serve and protect the shared interests of Ukrainian citizens in amateur radio communication and radiosport. The core aims of the organisation are to support the creative, non-commercial amateur work of its members in the field of radio, and to advance radiosport together with scientific and technical creativity.
