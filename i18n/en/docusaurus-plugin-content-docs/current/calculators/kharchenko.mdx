# Kharchenko (BiQuad) antenna calculator

:::info[About]
This calculator works out the dimensions of a Kharchenko antenna (the zigzag BiQuad
antenna). The Kharchenko is a high-gain directional antenna consisting of two square loops
and a metal reflector. It is widely used for Wi-Fi, GSM, LTE and other applications.
:::

<Calculator />

:::warning[Notes]
- **Standard frequencies:** pick one from the list of common frequencies or enter your own by hand.
- **Input impedance:** the standard values of 50 Ω and 75 Ω are supported.
- **Units:** results can be shown in millimetres, centimetres or metres.
- **Frequency range:** the calculator is optimised for the 400–3000 MHz range.
- **Reflector:** made from sheet metal or fine metal mesh.
- **Loop material:** use copper wire of the appropriate diameter (see the AWG size).
- **Build accuracy:** to achieve the best performance, keep to the calculated dimensions within ±1 mm.
- **Feed:** the coaxial cable connects at the centre of the lower loop, keeping to the calculated gap.
- **Polarisation:** the antenna is linearly polarised in the plane of the loops.
- **Gain:** typical gain is 10–12 dBi depending on frequency and build quality.
- **Radiation pattern:** the beamwidth is roughly 60–70 degrees.
- **Assembly:** the reflector must sit at exactly the calculated distance from the plane of the loops.
:::
