# J-pole antenna calculator

:::info[About]
This calculator works out the dimensions of a J-pole antenna (the J antenna).
The J-pole is a popular type of vertical antenna for the VHF and UHF bands.
:::

<Calculator />

:::warning[Notes]
- **Standard frequencies:** pick one from the list of common frequencies or enter your own by hand.
- **Units:** results can be shown in millimetres, centimetres or metres.
- **Dimension A** — the length of the upper radiating element (3/4 λ).
- **Dimension B** — the length of the lower element (1/4 λ).
- **Distance C** — the distance from the feed point to the lower end.
- **Dimension D** — an extra dimension used for tuning.
- The velocity factor is calculated automatically from the frequency and the wire diameter.
- The wire diameter affects the velocity factor and the overall dimensions of the antenna.
- For best results, use wire of 2–6 mm diameter.
- The antenna can be built from wire, tubing or metal strip.
:::
