# About us

The "Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators" community was founded in 2024 by two radio
amateurs, military signallers and war veterans — Leonid Pavliuchenko (UT3UTX) and
Vitalii Lazebnyk (UT0UI).

UARO started out as a local group of users of the Kyiv amateur radio repeaters R3, R81
and K2. Over the course of 2024 it grew exponentially, and its membership spread beyond
Ukraine.

In early 2025 we were granted official status as a non-governmental organisation. The
UARO NGO has set itself an ambitious goal — to become the driving force behind
popularising amateur radio among young people, to carry out research and educational
work, to write and publish material on radio communication, and to support the army by
training signals specialists.
