# Joining the UARO NGO

### Any licensed radio amateur can become a member of UARO.

:::warning[Requirements for candidate members]
Before joining, a candidate is required to read the articles of association, to abide by
them thereafter, and to take an active part in the work of the organisation towards its
statutory objectives.
:::

:::info[How to apply]
1. Print out the application form and the consent form.
2. Fill in by hand and sign the [application form](@site/src/pages/docs/membership.pdf) (page 1).
3. Fill in by hand and sign the [consent to the processing of personal data](@site/src/pages/docs/membership.pdf) (page 2).
4. Scan or photograph the documents.
5. Attach a 3×4 photo.
6. Send everything to: uaro.org.ua@gmail.com
:::
