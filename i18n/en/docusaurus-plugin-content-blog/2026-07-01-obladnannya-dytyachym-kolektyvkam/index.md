Equipment from the Swedish club SK7RN is already finding its new owners 🫡

<!-- truncate -->

Earlier we spoke about the help from the Swedish club SK7RN in the form of boxes of equipment. Well, all this equipment is already finding its new owners — children's and youth club stations, clubs and sports schools. The UX6FZZ team was chosen for a reason: it is the most active children's club station thanks to its tireless leader Mykola Lavretsia UX0FF. The club was given an HF transceiver, several handhelds and other pleasant bonuses. And this is only the beginning :)
P.S. If you are a leader of a club station or a group — contact the admins with a request, we will be glad to help!
