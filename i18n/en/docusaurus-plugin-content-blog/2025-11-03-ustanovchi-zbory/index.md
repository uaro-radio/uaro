🫡 Founding meeting of the NGO «Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators»

<!-- truncate -->

On 3 November 2025 the online founding meeting of the UARO NGO took place. The event was an important milestone in shaping the organisation's structure and defining the directions of its development.

On the agenda:
— new members were admitted to the organisation;
— the structure of departments was approved and heads appointed:
IT and digital transformation — Petro UR3PKI
Sport — Serhii UX2CT
Education and youth work — Kostiantyn UT2UCN
SMM — Luiza UR1CED
Community Relations (engagement with the community and its coordination) —
Andrii UR5LLL, Serhii UR5KET / OK8KET, Artem UW5EOW, Ihor UT0UG, Serhii UR3QJ

Club radio station:
We have come a long way to this — and now we are one step closer to our dream. Our own club radio station «Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators» is being set up at the Educational and Research Institute of Telecommunication Systems of the Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute. It will become a place for meetings, learning and joint work on technical and amateur radio projects. New ideas will be born here, young operators will grow, and the community we are all proud of will take shape.

UARO continues to develop educational, technical and communication initiatives, uniting Ukrainian radio amateurs around modern technology and shared goals.
