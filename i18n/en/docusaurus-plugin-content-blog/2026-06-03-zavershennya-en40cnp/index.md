Our activity with the special callsign EN40CNP has recently ended!

<!-- truncate -->

The on-air operation was dedicated to the 40th anniversary of the Chornobyl NPP tragedy and organised by the UARO NGO as part of the Chornobyl-40 award programme.
Over two weeks (from 18 to 30 April) we made 1565 QSOs with 111 countries in SSB, CW and FT8. At weekends we even gathered hour-long pile-ups on 20 m! 🌍
At first we planned a mini-expedition to the Exclusion Zone, but because of the fighting we could not get permission, so we worked to plan B — from home QTHs.
Special respect to Petro (UR3PKI) for the convenient chatbot that instantly reflected our activity on the qrz.com page.
