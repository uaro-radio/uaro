🇺🇦🇸🇪 UARO's cooperation with Sweden's radio amateur community

<!-- truncate -->

SM0AVX Tommy from Järfälla was the first to respond to our appeal for donations to Ukraine and arrived at Operation Change in Sollentuna with radio equipment. Tommy brought, among other things, a Heathkit SB-101 HF transceiver, a Tektronix oscilloscope, a rotator and a few other items. In the photo he has just unloaded them on the loading ramp. Tommy is also one of the most active members of the SK7RN CW group.

Thank you for your efforts, Tommy. I hope many others will follow your example and pass on their unused equipment to radio friends in Ukraine.

All the equipment will be handed over to the UARO radio club at KPI.
