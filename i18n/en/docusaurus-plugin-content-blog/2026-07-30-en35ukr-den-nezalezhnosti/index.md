Dear friends! On the occasion of the 35th anniversary of Ukraine's independence, the NGO «Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators», the NGO «All-Ukrainian Radio Amateur Emergency Union» and the NGO «Zaporizhzhia Radio Club» have established a commemorative award and the operation of the special callsign EN35UKR. The special callsign will be on the air from 1 to 31 August on the 80, 40, 30, 20, 17, 15, 12, 10, 2 m and 70 cm bands in all permitted modes; the operation will be carried out by a team of radio amateurs from all corners of Ukraine. The award conditions are simple: 35 QSOs made with the special callsign on different bands using different modes. Repeats on different days are allowed. You can follow the callsign's activity on the air on the qrz.com page (https://www.qrz.com/db/EN35UKR) or on uahamaward.com (https://uahamaward.com/awards/019f95c0-1e60-7209-9248-c12a3911bbc1). Those who wish may contact the organisers to exchange QSL cards for a completed contact. See you on the air. Glory to Ukraine!
