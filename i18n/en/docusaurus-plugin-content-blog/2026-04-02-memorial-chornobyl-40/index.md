Attention! The «CHORNOBYL-40» MEMORIAL: 18.04.2026 – 27.04.2026.
For the 40th anniversary of the Chornobyl NPP accident, the «Delta» radio club, the League of Radio Amateurs of Ukraine and the UARO NGO have established the «Chornobyl-40» award.
To meet the award's conditions, between 18 and 27 April 2026 (inclusive) you need to collect 40 points by making radio contacts or observations with participants of the «Chornobyl-40» Memorial.
Photos and the award conditions are on the site:
https://deltaclub.org.ua/novosti/diplom-chornobil-40.html#section6

Our callsign is EN40CNP, see you on the air!
