The «Telegraphy for beginners» project has launched

<!-- truncate -->

The UARO team has begun active work on a training kit designed to make the first steps in learning telegraphy as simple as possible — an art that demands patience, attention and inspiration.

The kit will include a simple iambic key, an emulator board, the necessary cables and software for Android. It will also come with the Morse alphabet and learning materials that help master telegraphy even from scratch.

We plan to give these kits free of charge to children's radio clubs, as well as to anyone who supports the project with a donation.
