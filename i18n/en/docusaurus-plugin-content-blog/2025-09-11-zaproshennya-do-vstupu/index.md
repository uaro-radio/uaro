👋 Dear colleagues and friends!
We invite you to join the NGO «Ukrainian Amateur Radio Operators». This is a great chance to become part of a community that unites like-minded people and develops amateur radio in Ukraine.

<!-- truncate -->

✅ To apply, follow this [link](https://uaro.org.ua/membership)

✅ After the next founding meeting and confirmation of membership, each member will be issued a membership card of the public organisation.

✅ Before joining, please read the organisation's Charter and be ready to take part in our shared work.

✅ Any licensed radio amateur can join.

ℹ️ The card is produced for a symbolic fee (roughly up to 100 UAH).
