You rejoice and marvel...
...at the generosity of radio amateurs from all over the country who are handing equipment to our friends in Ukraine. And they even cover great distances to deliver it to Operation Change. In the photo the head of Op-Change, Göran Skoglund, receives this week's «harvest» in Sollentuna from, among others, SM5FXK/Ulf and SMØEOQ/Bo. Great thanks to you and to all the other donors, not least those who handed the shipment to the terminal in Nybro via SM7OHE/Rolf in Färjestaden.
By the way, I heard that the first pallet of radio equipment is already at the border with Ukraine.
But radio clubs in Ukraine have great needs, so take a look at how you too can help.
73 de SMØDMY
